# Ozyn

**Your AI Productivity Assistant — Finish What's Left, Every Day.**

Ozyn is an intelligent AI assistant designed to make your day more productive. It helps you stay on top of tasks, close out what's been left unfinished, and build momentum — so you end each day with a sense of accomplishment.

## Why Ozyn?

We all have days where things slip through the cracks — a follow-up forgotten, a task half-done, a goal pushed to tomorrow. Ozyn bridges that gap by intelligently tracking what's left and helping you finish it.

## Key Features

- **Task Completion Engine** — Identifies unfinished work and helps you close it out efficiently.
- **Daily Productivity Flow** — Structures your day so high-priority items get done first.
- **Smart Reminders** — Gentle, context-aware nudges for tasks you might have overlooked.
- **Progress Tracking** — See what you've accomplished and what still needs attention.
- **Seamless Integration** — Works alongside your existing tools and workflows.

## How It Works

1. **Connect** — Ozyn integrates with your calendar, tasks, and communication tools.
2. **Analyze** — It identifies what's been started but not finished, and what needs your attention.
3. **Act** — Ozyn helps you prioritize, focus, and execute — so you finish what's left.

## Get Started

Ozyn is designed to fit into your workflow with minimal setup. Start using it today and turn unfinished tasks into completed ones.
