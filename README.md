# Ozyn

**Your AI Productivity Assistant — Finish What's Left, Every Day.**

Ozyn is an intelligent AI assistant designed to make your day more productive. It helps you stay on top of tasks, close out what's been left unfinished, and build momentum — so you end each day with a sense of accomplishment.

---

## What is Ozyn?

Ozyn is a smart productivity companion that bridges the gap between intention and execution. It doesn't just help you plan your day — it helps you **finish what you started**. By intelligently tracking incomplete tasks, surfacing forgotten follow-ups, and guiding you through a structured daily flow, Ozyn ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

Whether you're a busy professional juggling multiple projects, a team lead managing deliverables, or someone simply trying to build better habits — Ozyn adapts to your workflow and keeps you moving forward.

---

## Key Features

- **Task Completion Engine** — Identifies unfinished work and helps you close it out efficiently.
- **Daily Productivity Flow** — Structures your day so high-priority items get done first.
- **Smart Reminders** — Gentle, context-aware nudges for tasks you might have overlooked.
- **Progress Tracking** — See what you've accomplished and what still needs attention.
- **Seamless Integration** — Works alongside your existing tools and workflows without disruption.

## How It Works

1. **Connect** — Ozyn integrates with your calendar, task manager, and communication tools to understand your commitments.
2. **Analyze** — It identifies what's been started but not finished, what's overdue, and what needs your attention most.
3. **Act** — Ozyn helps you prioritize, focus, and execute — so you finish what's left and end each day with clarity.
